Think of this not as a wiki, but as a **sonar map of a dark ocean**. We ping the depths; we record the echoes; we never claim to have seen the fish. Each entry in this repository is a hypothesis supported by *visual evidence*, *timeline cross-referencing*, and *thematic resonance*. We are not here to win arguments; we are here to *extend the dream*.

This project welcomes **lore-cartographers**, **gameplay engineers**, **fan-theorists**, and **silent loremasters**. Whether you are here to debate the significance of the girl's hair color or to build a 3D model of the game's frozen moon, you have found your home base.

## 🧭 The Core Tenets of Pragmata

Before we descend into specifics, we must establish the **three laws of Pragmatic Reading**—the philosophical underpinning of every file in this repository.

1.  **The Principle of Absence**: What is *not* shown is always more important than what is. A missing texture is not a bug; it is a window into the Void. We document these voids.
2.  **The Principle of Lunar Gravity**: The moon is not a backdrop. It is a *character*. Its phases dictate motivation. Its tides pull at the narrative. We treat it with the reverence it deserves.
3.  **The Principle of Equivalence**: A sound effect and a line of dialogue carry equal weight. A footstep in the sand is a paragraph. A glitch in the matrix is a chapter. We transcribe all frequencies.

These tenets are non-negotiable. They are the compass by which we navigate the white noise of the internet's speculation and the white space of the game's own silence.

## 👤 Character Cartography: The Silent Signifiers

Without a single spoken line of dialogue, the *Pragmata* cast manages to say everything. This section of PRAGMARA is dedicated to deconstructing the **visual grammar** of each character.

### The Astronaut (He/They, Presumed)

- **Visual Signature**: The bulky, utilitarian suit contrasted with the child-like red visor glow.
- **Cartographic Role**: *The Anchor*. This character represents stability in a chaotic orbit. Our documentation focuses on the subtle shifts in his posture between trailers—from rigid to fatigued.
- **Mechanical Read**: His animations suggest a weight system that extends beyond physics. Is he carrying grief? Or is the planet simply too heavy? We have vectors for both.

### The Girl (She/Her, Confirmed by Marketing)

- **Visual Signature**: The plain white dress against the industrial wasteland. A symbol of organic life in a mechanical graveyard.
- **Cartographic Role**: *The Catalyst*. She moves the plot, not through action, but through presence.
- **Mechanical Read**: Her "simulated" nature, hinted at in early footage, is a labyrinth of rabbit holes. We have archived every frame of her flickering animations across all public footage.

### The Accidental Third (Visionary)

- **Visual Signature**: Shadows that don't match the light source; a reflection that lingers a fraction of a second too long.
- **Cartographic Role**: *The Variable*.
- **Mechanical Read**: This is less a character and more a *phenomenon*. We catalog instances of "impossible geometry" in the game's world that might hint at a second player, or an invasive entity.

## 🗺️ Community Cartography: How to Contribute

PRAGMARA is a crowd-sourced map of an uncharted territory. Here’s how you can leave your own flag on the moon.

1.  **File a Fracture Report**: Found a glitch, a visual anomaly, or a timeline inconsistency? Submit a detailed report. Include frame timestamps and a description of your hypothesis.
2.  **Vote on Consensus**: Existing theories need your ruling. Use the emoji reactions to signal agreement (`👍`) or dissent (`👎`). High-consensus theories are highlighted as "Seismic" shifts in the lore.
3.  **Create a "Deep Dive"**: Write a long-form analysis of a single mechanic or character trait. Format it as a Markdown file and submit it for review. If it is coherent, it is added to the main index.
4.  **Code the Map**: If you are a developer, you can help us build the *Chrono-Viewer*—a tool that visualizes the Fracture Database on a 4D timeline.

We do not gate-keep based on seniority. If you have a **quality, well-argued theory**, you are a *Cartographer*. That is the only rank that matters.
